# southern
Australia's open-source artificial intelligence initiative, founded by Manish Mathew.


## Open AI from Australia, built for the world

Southern is an Australian open-source artificial intelligence initiative
founded by **Manish Mathew**.

Southern is dedicated to elders, knowledge keepers and communities who have
preserved the world's languages, knowledge, cultures and heritage across
generations.

We acknowledge Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples as the First
Peoples of Australia and recognise their continuing connections to Country,
cultures, languages and knowledge systems.

Southern aims to build open, transparent, secure and globally significant
artificial intelligence through responsible research, engineering and
community collaboration.

## Founding objectives

- Build open AI models and research tools
- Establish Australian AI benchmarks
- Develop transparent training and evaluation systems
- Support the preservation of languages and cultural heritage
- Advance Australian sovereign AI capability
- Make AI research accessible to everyone
