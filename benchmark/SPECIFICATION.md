# Southern Benchmark v0.1

Southern Benchmark is an open evaluation framework for measuring how well AI systems perform on Australian, technical, reasoning, safety and culturally sensitive tasks.

## Initial categories

- Australian knowledge
- Australian English
- Coding
- Reasoning
- Safety and hallucination resistance

## Benchmark principles

- Clear provenance
- Reproducible evaluation
- Model-neutral testing
- Transparent scoring
- Licence-aware data
- Privacy protection
- Cultural respect
- Human-reviewed reference answers

## Initial target

The first release will contain 500 reviewed benchmark items:

- 100 Australian knowledge
- 100 Australian English
- 100 coding
- 100 reasoning
- 100 safety
